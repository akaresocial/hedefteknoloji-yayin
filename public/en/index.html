01Vehicle cutaway

One vehicle. Every system. One partner.

From the diagnostic port to the engine’s brain, from the emissions line to the steering: for every system on the vehicles that come into your workshop, we bring you the right brand and the expertise behind it.

01Vehicle cutaway

Section scan

  1. 01/06 Diagnostic port (OBD)

    Diagnostic tools reach every control unit in the vehicle through this port: fault codes, live data, service functions and programming.

  2. 02/06 Engine control unit (ECU)

    Reading and writing engine software, chip tuning and performance optimisation.

  3. 03/06 Steering (EPS / EHPS)

    A bench tester for electric (EPS) and electro-hydraulic (EHPS) steering racks that pinpoints faults at component level.

  4. 04/06 DPF and EGR

    Emulator and workshop solutions for diesel particulate filter and exhaust gas recirculation faults.

  5. 05/06 AdBlue / SCR

    Emulators for faults in the AdBlue tank, dosing unit and SCR catalyst.

  6. 06/06 Wiring harness

    Wiring diagrams, component locations and technical data subscriptions for getting the repair right.
