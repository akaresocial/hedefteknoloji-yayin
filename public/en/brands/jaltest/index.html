Jaltest heavy-duty and off-highway diagnostics

Jaltest is the diagnostics solution developed by Spanish manufacturer Cojali for commercial vehicles, agricultural and construction machinery. From trucks and buses to tractors, excavators and marine engines, it combines diagnostics, wiring diagrams and repair guidance in one software. See the product range on jaltesttr.com.
